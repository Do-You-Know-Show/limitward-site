How it works

  • Risk-reward = |target − entry| ÷ |entry − stop|.
  • Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + risk-reward), before costs.
  • Expectancy (in R) = win rate × risk-reward − (1 − win rate) × 1, assuming your average loss is 1R.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate risk-reward ratio?

Divide the distance from entry to target by the distance from entry to stop. A $2 stop and a $5 target is 2.5, written 1 : 2.5.

What win rate do I need to break even?

Break-even win rate equals 1 divided by (1 + risk-reward), before costs. At 1 : 2 it is about 33%, and at 1 : 1 it is 50%.

What is expectancy?

The average result per trade in units of risk (R): win rate times average win, minus loss rate times average loss. This calculator assumes your average loss is 1R.

Do fees and slippage change the result?

Yes. Costs reduce your wins and increase your losses, so the real break-even win rate is higher than the figure shown.
