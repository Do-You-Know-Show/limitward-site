Privacy policy

Short version: the calculators run in your browser and send nothing to us. We only store an email address if you join the waitlist.

Calculators

The numbers you enter into the calculators stay on your device. They are not sent to any server and are not stored.

Waitlist

  • What we collect: your email address, the page you signed up from, the date and time, the version of the consent text you agreed to, and a one-way hash of your IP address (we cannot recover the IP address from it) used only to limit abuse.
  • Why: to email you about the launch of Limitward. We do not sell or share your email address.
  • Who runs it: Sarang AI Technologies Inc., an Alberta, Canada company. The data is stored with our hosting provider, Railway, on servers that may be outside Canada.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe, or until the product is launched or abandoned, whichever comes first.
  • Your choices: you can withdraw consent and have your address deleted at any time. Every email we send will contain a one-click unsubscribe link that deletes your address.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies and does not currently run analytics.

An app that keeps you inside your own rules

We are building a phone app that checks every trade against your risk rules before you place it, adds a cooldown after losses, and shows what rule-breaking cost you. Join the waitlist for early access.

Limitward calculators are educational tools that do arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They are not investment, tax or legal advice, and they do not recommend any trade. Trading involves a risk of loss. Firm rules change often: always confirm the current rules with your firm before relying on a result.
