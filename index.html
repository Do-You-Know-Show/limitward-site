How it works

  1. Enter your numbers. Account size, how much you will risk, and where your stop goes.
  2. See your size and your limit. The exact position size, the dollars at risk, and how many stop-outs your drawdown can absorb.
  3. Trade inside your plan. Copy or print a daily plan, or share a link to your setup.

A quick example

A $50,000 account risking 1% is $500. If your stop is $2.50 away per share, the size is 200 shares ($500 ÷ $2.50). The calculators do the same for futures ticks, forex pips and fees.
