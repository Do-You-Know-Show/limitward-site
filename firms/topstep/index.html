How "safe risk per trade" is worked out

It takes the room between your equity and the binding floor (the closer of the max-loss and daily-loss limits), uses only the share you choose (50% by default), and splits it across the number of full stop-outs you want to survive in a row (2 by default). This is a conservative planning aid, not a prediction or a recommendation.

Topstep Combine rules in plain English

  • Monitored in real time, but the floor only updates at the end of each trading day.
  • Both realized and unrealized P&L count toward the limit.
  • On breach the Combine account is liquidated and you must Reset before funding.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Topstep Maximum Loss Limit work?

In the Trading Combine the Maximum Loss Limit starts $2,000, $3,000 or $4,500 below the account size (for the $50K, $100K and $150K accounts). It rises as your end-of-day balance grows and never moves down.

Do open (unrealized) losses count toward the Topstep limit?

Yes. According to Topstep, both realized and unrealized profit and loss count toward the Maximum Loss Limit, so an open loss that reaches the limit can trigger liquidation.

When does the Topstep Maximum Loss Limit stop trailing?

In the Trading Combine it locks permanently once it reaches your starting balance.
