How "safe risk per trade" is worked out

It takes the room between your equity and the binding floor (the closer of the max-loss and daily-loss limits), uses only the share you choose (50% by default), and splits it across the number of full stop-outs you want to survive in a row (2 by default). This is a conservative planning aid, not a prediction or a recommendation.

Topstep Express Funded rules in plain English

  • The balance starts at $0, so the limit starts at -$2,000, -$3,000 or -$4,500 for the $50K, $100K and $150K accounts. This calculator uses the account size as the starting balance, which gives the same distances.
  • The limit locks at $0 (your starting balance in this calculator) once your balance reaches the starting limit amount, and also after your first payout. After a payout, use Custom rules with a static limit if you need to model it.
  • Both realized and unrealized P&L count toward the limit. On breach the Express Funded Account is permanently closed (Topstep says a Back2Funded option may be available if you are eligible).

Frequently asked questions

How is the Topstep Express Funded limit different from the Combine?

The mechanics are the same end-of-day trailing limit, but the Express Funded Account balance starts at $0 and the limit starts below it. It locks at $0 once your balance reaches the starting limit amount, and also after your first payout.

What happens if I breach the limit on an Express Funded Account?

According to Topstep, the account is permanently closed. A Back2Funded option may be available if you are eligible.

Do unrealized losses count on the Express Funded Account?

Yes. Topstep says both realized and unrealized profit and loss count toward the Maximum Loss Limit.
