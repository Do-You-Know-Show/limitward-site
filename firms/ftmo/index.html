How "safe risk per trade" is worked out

It takes the room between your equity and the binding floor (the closer of the max-loss and daily-loss limits), uses only the share you choose (50% by default), and splits it across the number of full stop-outs you want to survive in a row (2 by default). This is a conservative planning aid, not a prediction or a recommendation.

FTMO rules in plain English

  • Daily loss is measured on equity: balance + open P/L ± swaps – commissions.
  • The max loss floor is static: it does not move up with profits.
  • The same percentages apply to the Challenge and Verification phases. The 1-Step and Instant products use different numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the FTMO Max Daily Loss on the 2-Step Challenge?

It is 5% of the initial simulated capital. The limit is recalculated daily at 00:00 CE(S)T, starting from your account balance at that time.

Is the FTMO daily loss measured on balance or equity?

Equity. FTMO measures it as balance plus open positions profit and loss, plus or minus swaps, minus commissions.

Is the FTMO Max Loss static or trailing?

On the 2-Step Challenge it is a static 10% of the initial simulated capital. It does not move up with profits. Other FTMO products use different numbers, so check the product you bought.
