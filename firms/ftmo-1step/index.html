How "safe risk per trade" is worked out

It takes the room between your equity and the binding floor (the closer of the max-loss and daily-loss limits), uses only the share you choose (50% by default), and splits it across the number of full stop-outs you want to survive in a row (2 by default). This is a conservative planning aid, not a prediction or a recommendation.

FTMO 1-Step rules in plain English

  • Daily loss is measured on equity, and it is recalculated at 00:00 CE(S)T from your balance at that time.
  • The max loss limit trails your end-of-day balance: it is based on your highest 00:00 CE(S)T balance, or the initial capital if that is higher.
  • FTMO says that when you withdraw a Reward the Maximum Loss Limit resets to 90% of the initial capital. Account sizes on this product may differ from the list here: edit the starting balance if yours is different.

Frequently asked questions

What are the FTMO 1-Step loss limits?

According to FTMO, the Max Daily Loss is 3% of the initial simulated capital and the Max Loss is 10%, which is an end-of-day trailing limit based on your highest 00:00 CE(S)T balance.

How is the FTMO 1-Step different from the 2-Step?

The 2-Step Challenge uses a 5% daily loss and a static 10% max loss. The 1-Step uses a 3% daily loss and a 10% max loss that trails your end-of-day balance.

Is the FTMO daily loss measured on balance or equity?

Equity. If equity drops below the limit, FTMO considers the rule violated.
