Futures tick values

To size a futures trade you need the dollar value of each tick. Tick value is the contract multiplier times the tick size.

Confirm tick sizes and values on your broker's or the exchange's contract specification page before you rely on them. Contracts and their specifications can change.

From points to dollars

Number of ticks = stop distance ÷ tick size. Dollars per contract = ticks × tick value. For ES, a 4-point stop is 16 ticks, and 16 × $12.50 = $200 per contract. The same 4 points on MES, the micro contract, is $20.

Micros versus minis

Micro contracts are a tenth of the size of their E-mini counterparts, which lets you size a trade more precisely when your risk budget is small. The multiplier is the only difference in the formula.

Frequently asked questions

What is the tick value of ES?

ES moves in 0.25-point ticks, each worth $12.50. That is a $50 multiplier times 0.25.

What is the tick value of MNQ?

MNQ moves in 0.25-point ticks worth $0.50 each, from a $2 multiplier times 0.25.

How do I convert points to dollars?

Divide the points by the tick size to get ticks, then multiply by the tick value. Multiply by the number of contracts for the total.
