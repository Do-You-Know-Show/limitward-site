Position size calculator

Decide how much you are willing to lose first. This works out the size that keeps a stop-out inside that amount, rounded down, with fees included.

How the maths works

  • Stocks: size = (risk − commissions) ÷ (|entry − stop| + per-share costs), rounded down.
  • Futures: tick value = contract multiplier × tick size. Risk per contract = stop distance in ticks × tick value + round-trip fees.
  • Forex: pip value per unit = pip size × quote-to-account rate. Units = risk ÷ (stop pips × pip value per unit).
  • Crypto: quantity = risk ÷ (|entry − stop| + fees per unit). Margin = position value ÷ leverage.
  • Options: long options risk the premium × 100 per contract. A credit spread risks (width − credit) × 100 per contract.

Size is always rounded down so the result never exceeds your risk limit. Exchange specifications such as tick values should be confirmed against your broker or the exchange. For worked examples see the position size guide and the futures tick values guide.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate position size?

Divide the dollars you are willing to risk by the dollars you lose per unit if your stop is hit, and round down. For futures, the dollars per contract come from the stop distance in ticks times the tick value, plus fees.

Does the calculator include fees?

Yes. You can enter commissions, per-share costs, round-trip futures fees or percentage fees on crypto. They are added to the cost of a stop-out before the size is rounded down.

Is the information I type sent anywhere?

No. The calculations run in your browser and the numbers you enter are not sent to any server.

Which markets does it cover?

Stocks, futures, forex, crypto, long options and defined-risk credit spreads.
