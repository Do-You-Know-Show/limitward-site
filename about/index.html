About Limitward

Limitward makes free calculators that show traders how much they can lose before they place a trade.

Why it exists

Many blown accounts come from a position that was too big, or from a daily or trailing limit nobody did the maths on. These tools do that arithmetic in seconds.

How we keep the rules accurate

  • Every prop-firm rule set is read from the firm's own published page, and the date it was last checked is shown on the page.
  • The numbers in the guides come from the same code as the calculators, which is covered by automated tests.
  • Firms change rules often. If something looks wrong or out of date, email us and we will fix it.

Your privacy

The calculators run in your browser. What you type is not sent to us, and there are no cookies and no analytics. Details are in the privacy policy.

Who we are

Limitward is built by Sarang AI Technologies Inc., an Alberta, Canada company. We are not a broker, a prop firm or a financial adviser, and we are not affiliated with any firm named on this site. Nothing here is investment advice.
