How "safe risk per trade" is worked out

It takes the room between your equity and the binding floor (the closer of the max-loss and daily-loss limits), uses only the share you choose (50% by default), and splits it across the number of full stop-outs you want to survive in a row (2 by default). This is a conservative planning aid, not a prediction or a recommendation.

Firm presets show the date they were last checked against the firm's own pages. If your firm or product is not listed, choose Custom rules.

Frequently asked questions

What does the prop-firm drawdown calculator show?

Your loss-limit floor, how much room you have before it, which limit is binding (maximum loss or daily loss), and a per-trade risk that keeps a short run of stop-outs inside that room.

Which firms are included?

Topstep (Trading Combine) and FTMO (2-Step Challenge), each checked against the firm's own page, plus a custom option for other rules.

How is safe risk per trade calculated?

By default it uses half of your remaining room, split across two full stop-outs in a row. You can change both numbers. It is a planning aid, not a recommendation.

Are the firm rules always current?

Firms change their rules. Each firm page shows the date we last checked it against the firm's own page, and you should always confirm with your firm.
